Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Marrow handles information when you use our web marketing desk.

1. What we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email, brand info).
  • Content you upload or generate (scripts, media, drafts).
  • Connected social tokens (for example TikTok access and refresh tokens) stored so we can publish when you ask.
  • Basic product usage needed to run the desk.

2. How we use it

We use this information to run Marrow: draft content, keep your workspace, connect channels, and post only after you approve. We do not sell your personal information.

3. TikTok and other platforms

When you connect TikTok, you authorize Marrow to receive basic profile info and to publish videos you approve. Tokens are stored on our servers. You can disconnect TikTok in Marrow to stop publishing.

4. Sharing

We share data with processors that help run the product (hosting, database, AI model providers) under agreements that limit use to providing the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Retention

We keep account and content data while your workspace is active. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect tokens and content. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact email on your Marrow account or the email listed with this deployment.
